Kimchi Fried Rice

This was one of the first meals I have made by myself many years ago. At this point in time I was only able to successfully make ramen which shows how easy this recipe is.

Ingredients

  • rice
  • kimch
  • spam

Steps

  1. have rice prepared
  2. dice vegetables and spam

Miso Soup

Fastest and easiest soup to make. Great for breakfast!

Ingredients

  • white miso paste
  • green onions
  • dashi
  • tofu
  • wakeme seaweed

Steps

  1. bring water and seaweed to a boil
  2. add miso paste, dashi, and tofu
  3. add green onions

Chicken Nuggets with Yangnyeom Chicken Sauce Dip

I totally improved this one but the sauce tastes fantastic with the chicken, and it is much easier to make and healthier than korean fried chicken.

Ingredients

  • gochujang
  • ketchup
  • honey
  • avocado oil
  • sesamee seeds
  • premade chicken nuggies or chicken katsu (frozen)

Steps

  1. air fry the breaded chicken
  2. mix sauce
